Salt Shed Backline

The Salt Shed is a Chicago venue built inside a former industrial salt warehouse, with an indoor room and an outdoor stage. This page covers the house backline and production, what touring acts usually carry, and the advance details production managers ask about most.

About this listing: The Salt Shed is a real venue, but Greenroom Atlas is fictional. The gear, capacities, contacts and policies on this page were written for a class project and have not been confirmed with the venue. Do not use them to advance a real show.
Quick answer: In our sample profile, the indoor room provides a five-piece drum kit with hardware, two bass rigs, two guitar combos, a stage piano and 48 stage inputs. Cymbals, guitars and specialty keyboards are not included and should be carried or rented.

What to bring or rent

  • Cymbals, snare preferences and any electronic drum pads.
  • Guitar and bass instruments, pedalboards, stands and spare strings.
  • Synths, samplers and playback rigs, along with a backup laptop.
  • Your own wireless and in-ear packs if you need more than four wireless channels.
  • Gaff tape, labeled cables and a printed stage plot for local crew.

Chicago has several backline rental shops that deliver to the venue dock. Book rentals at least two weeks ahead for weekend shows, since summer festival season ties up most of the city's inventory.

Production tips

Large rooms reward a tight advance. Confirm which bass rig and drum riser position you get when sharing a bill with another touring act, and ask the production manager for the current wireless frequency plan before you arrive. Outdoor shows can be held for weather, so build a lightning delay plan into your day sheet and know where the gear will be covered.
