Gabe's Iowa City Capacity

Gabe's is a two-level club in downtown Iowa City that has hosted touring punk, indie and hip-hop acts for decades. Here is how many people each room holds, and what you need to know to advance a show there.

About this listing: Gabe's is a real venue, but Codex Venue is fictional. Every capacity figure, contact, policy and fee on this page was written for a class project and has not been confirmed with the venue. Do not use it to book or advance a real show.
Quick answer: Our sample profile lists the upstairs showroom at 250 standing and the downstairs bar at 120, for a combined building capacity of 370. Most touring shows play upstairs.

Capacity by room

RoomStanding capacityRecommended sellTypical use
Upstairs showroom250220Touring headliners and local bills with three or four acts
Downstairs bar120100Acoustic sets, DJ nights and early shows before an upstairs event
Full building370320Two-room festival nights and album release parties

The recommended sell leaves room for guest list, staff and a comfortable crowd near the bar. For an all-ages show, plan on about 10 percent fewer tickets, since the bar area is roped off and becomes harder to move through.

Load in and load out

Access
Curbside load-in on Washington Street; upstairs gear goes up one flight of stairs.
Timing
Earliest load-in 4:00 p.m.; doors usually 7:00 p.m.; curfew 1:00 a.m.
Vehicles
No bus parking at the door. Vans can park on the street after 6:00 p.m.
Plan for
Bring a hand truck and extra crew for heavy cabinets.

Ticketing

Platform
TixLoft, with a cash box office at the door on show night.
Settlement
Night of show, in cash or by check.
Comps
Ten guest list spots per show for the headliner, four for each support act.

Merch

Venue cut
No venue cut on soft goods or music in our sample profile.
Who sells
Band sells; the venue does not supply a merch seller.
Table
Back corner of the upstairs room, with one six-foot table and one outlet.

Backline and production

House gear
Drum shell pack with no cymbals, one bass amp and a 24-channel console.
Monitors
Four wedges on two mixes, run from front of house.
Engineer
House sound engineer included in the venue fee.

Hospitality

Green room
Small shared room behind the upstairs stage, with no shower.
Buyout
$15 per touring member in place of a hot meal.
Drinks
Two drink tickets per performer.

Routing notes

Iowa City shows draw heavily from University of Iowa students, so dates during finals week and summer break tend to sell slower. Routes that pair Iowa City with Des Moines, Chicago or Minneapolis keep drive times under five hours. If your act draws well in Cedar Rapids, see The Ledgerline Room on our accessible venues list as an alternate stop about 30 minutes north.
